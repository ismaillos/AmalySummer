Our Five Pillars

What AMALY Builds in Every Child

Each pillar answers a question every child is asking — shaping who they are, how they think, and what they give to the world.

🌟

Identity & Heritage

Who am I? Children discover their roots, values, and unique story — grounded in Moroccan and Arab heritage, open to the world.

🔭

Thinking & Discovery

How does the world work? Logic, chess, soroban, and science exploration sharpen the mind and nurture deep curiosity.

⚡

Creation & Innovation

What can I create? Crafts, coding, design, and AI projects transform children from consumers into builders.

🎯

Future Skills

How can I thrive? Communication, entrepreneurship, and leadership prepare children for a world we can't yet fully imagine.

🤝

Community & Impact

How can I help? SDGs, sustainability, and global citizenship connect children from Morocco to the world.

Summer Camp 2026

What Your Child Will Explore & Impact

5 weeks of discovery, creation, and growth. Each week has a big question children explore through missions, live sessions, creative projects, and community challenges.

Week 1

SDGs & Digital Explorers

How can we use technology to make the world better? Children explore the SDGs, AI, and digital literacy.

Week 2

Identity & Values

Who am I, and what values guide my actions? Identity passport, character, and emotional intelligence.

Week 3

Chess, Storytelling & AI

How can I think strategically and tell powerful stories? Chess, creative writing, and AI prompting.

Week 4

Culture, Calligraphy & Soroban

How can different cultures help us grow? Chinese discovery, Arabic calligraphy, and mental arithmetic.

Week 5

AMALY Festival

What have I discovered, created, and achieved? Showcase, celebrate, and launch your impact on the world.
