Summer Camp 2026

What Your Child Will Explore & Impact

5 weeks of discovery, creation, and growth. Each week has a big question children explore through missions, live sessions, creative projects, and community challenges.

Week 1

SDGs & Digital Explorers

How can we use technology to make the world better? Children explore the SDGs, AI, and digital literacy.

Week 2

Identity & Values

Who am I, and what values guide my actions? Identity passport, character, and emotional intelligence.

Week 3

Chess, Storytelling & AI

How can I think strategically and tell powerful stories? Chess, creative writing, and AI prompting.

Week 4

Culture, Calligraphy & Soroban

How can different cultures help us grow? Chinese discovery, Arabic calligraphy, and mental arithmetic.

Week 5

AMALY Festival

What have I discovered, created, and achieved? Showcase, celebrate, and launch your impact on the world.
