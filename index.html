Five Pillars of Growth

What Every Child Builds at AMALY

Five interconnected pillars — each week deepens one, and all five come together by the final Festival.

🌟

Identity & Values

Who they are, where they come from, and what they stand for — the foundation that makes everything else meaningful.

🧠

Critical Thinking

Chess, logic puzzles, and AI literacy train the mind to question, analyze, and decide — skills that last a lifetime.

🎨

Creativity & Making

Storytelling, calligraphy, DIY projects — children learn to express themselves and turn ideas into reality.

🚀

Leadership & Collaboration

Team challenges, live workshops, and community projects build the confidence to lead and the empathy to connect.

🌍

Global Citizenship

Rooted in Moroccan heritage, open to the world — children engage with the SDGs and discover how they can make a difference.
