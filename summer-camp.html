Why AMALY

A Summer That Changes More Than the Season

Most summer programs fill the time. AMALY fills the child. Our curriculum is grounded in four evidence-based frameworks — constructivism, project-based learning, socio-emotional learning (SEL), and identity-based pedagogy — endorsed by UNESCO and OECD Education 2030.

We start with the self: identity, values, and heritage. Then we expand outward to the world. Because children need self-understanding to ground their learning, and character to make their impact meaningful.

Every activity, every session, every challenge is designed with one question: who is this child becoming?

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions. See all FAQs →

What ages can participate? ▼
AMALY Summer Camp is designed for children aged 6 to 14. Activities are adapted by age group so every child is appropriately challenged and engaged.
What languages is the camp available in? ▼
The camp is available in Arabic, French, and English. Bilingual (Arabic/French or Arabic/English) and trilingual options are also available. You select your preference during registration.
How much time does it require each day? ▼
The camp is designed to be flexible. Daily live sessions run each day, with missions, projects, and challenges completed at your child’s own pace throughout the day.
What technology does my child need? ▼
A device (tablet, laptop, or computer) with a stable internet connection, a working camera and microphone for live sessions, and access to Google Classroom. We provide a dedicated @amaly.ma student account.
Can siblings register together? ▼
Yes! Each child needs a separate registration, but you can use the same parent information. Contact us after registering — we offer a family discount for 2 or more children from the same household.
What is the refund policy? ▼
Full refunds are available up to 14 days before the camp start date. After that, a 50% refund is available up to 7 days before start. No refund after the camp has started, but we can transfer enrollment to a future cohort.
Do you ship the Mission Box internationally? ▼
We ship within Morocco and to select countries including France, Belgium, Canada, UAE, and others. During registration you specify your location. Contact us if you’re unsure about your country.
When does the camp start? ▼
AMALY Summer Camp 2026 starts 29 June 2026 and runs for 5 weeks. Registration is open now — spots are limited and filled on a first-come, first-served basis.
