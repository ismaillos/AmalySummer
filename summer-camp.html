Your Child's Journey

What Your Child Will Explore & Impact

Every week, all five pillars come alive through a rich daily routine. Missions, live sessions, creative projects, and family challenges — woven into one continuous journey of growth.

Week 1

I Am AMALY

The journey begins with identity. Who am I? What do I stand for? Children discover their values, their story, and their place in the world.

1
2
Week 2

My Mind Is a Superpower

Chess, Soroban, logic puzzles, and critical thinking. The mind is a muscle — and this week is training day.

Week 3

I Create, Therefore I Am

Coding, AI, design, and hands-on making. Children shift from being users of technology to creators of it.

3
4
Week 4

The World Is My Classroom

Science, world geography, and cross-cultural discovery — with a focus on Morocco's heritage and global connections.

Week 5

Together We Rise

Collaboration, empathy, and peer leadership. Building real bonds with fellow campers across borders and languages.

5
6
Week 6

My Legacy Starts Here

Showcase, celebrate, and launch impact. Children present their summer portfolios and share their story with family and the world.
