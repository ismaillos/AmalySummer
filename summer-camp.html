Your Child's Journey

What Your Child Will Explore & Impact

5 weeks of discovery, creation, and growth. Every week has a big question that drives missions, live sessions, creative projects, and family challenges — all five pillars woven into one continuous journey.

Week 1

SDGs & Digital Explorers

How can we use technology to make the world a better place?

Children explore the 17 SDGs, discover AI, practice online safety, and create their own Digital Changemaker Poster using Canva.

🌍 SDGs💻 Digital Literacy🤖 AI Discovery🔒 Online Safety
1
2
Week 2

Identity & Values

Who am I, and what values guide my actions?

Identity passport, family tree, gratitude journal, Prophetic stories, and daily values challenges — children build a deep sense of self.

🌟 Identity❤️ Character📖 Heritage🤝 Emotional Intelligence
Week 3

Chess, Storytelling & AI

How can I think strategically and tell powerful stories?

Chess challenges and mini-tournament, creative story missions, character design, and AI prompting — thinking meets imagination.

♟ Chess📚 Storytelling💡 Creativity🤖 AI Prompting
3
4
Week 4

Culture, Calligraphy & Soroban

How can different cultures and learning tools help us grow?

Chinese songs, games and culture exploration, Arabic calligraphy practice, and Soroban mental arithmetic challenges.

🇨🇳 Chinese Discovery✍ Calligraphy🧮 Soroban🌏 Cultural Exploration
Week 5

AMALY Festival

What have I discovered, created, and achieved this summer?

Portfolio exhibition, chess finals, soroban finals, storytelling showcase, SDG presentations, and the Awards Ceremony — the grand finale.

🎉 Celebration🏆 Showcase🤝 Community🌟 Reflection
