6-Week Journey

Weekly Themes

A progressive journey from self-discovery to global impact — each week building on the last.

Week 1 · 29 June

I Am AMALY

Children begin with identity, values, and personal story. What makes me unique? What do I stand for?

1
2
Week 2 · 7 July

Think Like a Champion

Chess, Soroban, logic puzzles, and critical thinking skills. The mind is a muscle — let's train it.

Week 3 · 14 July

Future Skills Week

Coding, AI introduction, communication techniques, and the basics of entrepreneurial thinking.

4
Week 4 · 21 July

Explore the World

Science experiments, world geography, curiosity-driven discovery and cross-cultural learning.

Week 5 · 28 July

Friendship & Community

Collaboration, empathy, peer leadership, and building meaningful connections with fellow campers.

Week 6 · 4 August

Culture, Impact & Showcase

Celebrate the journey, SDG missions, final projects, certificates, and the grand showcase.
