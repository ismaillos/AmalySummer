Frequently Asked Questions

📚 About the Program
What is AMALY Summer Camp? ▼
AMALY Summer Camp 2026 is a 5-week online learning adventure for children aged 6–14. Running Summer 2026, it combines live workshops, missions, creative projects, brain training (chess, soroban, logic), and community building — all delivered through Google Classroom and WhatsApp.
What ages can participate? ▼
AMALY Summer Camp is designed for children aged 6 to 14 years old. Activities are adapted to different age groups within this range so every child is appropriately challenged and engaged.
What languages is the camp available in? ▼
The camp is available in Arabic, French, and English. You can also choose bilingual (Arabic/French or Arabic/English) or trilingual options. You select your preferred language during registration.
How much time does it require each day? ▼
The camp is designed to be flexible. Daily live sessions run each day, with additional missions, projects, and challenges that children complete at their own pace. Expert guest workshops happen occasionally throughout the camp.
What happens during the weekly themes? ▼
Each of the 5 weeks has a big question and dedicated theme: Week 1 (SDGs & Digital Explorers), Week 2 (Identity & Values), Week 3 (Chess, Storytelling & AI), Week 4 (Culture, Calligraphy & Soroban), Week 5 (AMALY Festival). Every week includes daily live sessions, with a consistent rhythm: Mission Launch (Mon), Discovery & Live Session (Tue), Live Workshop (Wed), Creative Project (Thu), Family Challenge (Fri), Club Session (Sat), Reflection (Sun).
📦 Packages
What's the difference between Basic and Basic+? ▼
Both packages include the full digital experience: live workshops, missions, challenges, projects, Google Classroom, WhatsApp community, portfolio, and certificate. The Basic+ package adds a physical Mission Box delivered to your home — containing printed activities, DIY materials, challenge cards, collectibles, and surprise gifts.
Can I upgrade from Basic to Basic+ later? ▼
Yes! You can upgrade from Basic to Basic+ before the shipping deadline (to be announced). Contact us at contact@amaly.ma to upgrade your package and arrange the additional payment and shipping details.
Can siblings register together? ▼
Yes! Each child needs a separate registration form, but you can use the same parent information. For siblings, please contact us after registering — we offer a family discount for 2 or more children from the same household.
💳 Payment
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept payment via online payment link (card) and bank transfer (RIB). After your registration is submitted, you'll receive an email with both payment options and detailed instructions.
When do I pay? ▼
Payment is requested after registration. Your spot is reserved with a PENDING PAYMENT status. Once we receive and validate your payment (within 24 hours), your status moves to PAID and you receive your welcome pack, WhatsApp community access, and Google Classroom details.
What is your refund policy? ▼
Full refunds are available up to 14 days before the camp start date (29 June). After that, we offer a 50% refund up to 7 days before start. No refund is available after the camp has started, but we can transfer your enrollment to a future cohort. Please contact us for any special circumstances.
💻 Technical Requirements
What technology does my child need? ▼
Your child needs a device (tablet, laptop, or computer) with a stable internet connection, a working camera and microphone for live sessions, and access to Google Classroom (via a browser or the Google Classroom app). We'll provide a dedicated @amaly.ma student Google account for each enrolled child.
Will my child get a Google account? ▼
Yes. After payment is confirmed, we create a dedicated student Google account (e.g., child001@amaly.ma) for each enrolled child. This account gives them access to Google Classroom, Google Meet for live sessions, Google Drive for their portfolio, and Google Calendar for the weekly schedule.
Is WhatsApp required? ▼
WhatsApp is our community platform for parents. You'll be invited to a dedicated AMALY community group where we share updates, resources, announcements, and family challenges. It's not strictly required, but strongly recommended for staying connected with the AMALY community.
🚚 Basic+ Logistics
When does the Mission Box get delivered? ▼
For Basic+ participants, Mission Boxes are shipped before the camp start date (29 June). The exact shipping timeline depends on your location. We'll communicate the deadline for address confirmation and expected delivery dates by email.
Do you ship internationally? ▼
We ship within Morocco and to select countries where we have diaspora communities (France, Belgium, Canada, UAE, and others). During registration, you'll specify your country and city. If you're unsure about international shipping to your country, please contact us before registering for Basic+.
What's inside the Mission Box? ▼
The Mission Box is a carefully curated kit containing: printed activities and workbooks, DIY craft and project materials aligned with each week's theme, challenge cards, collectible items, and surprise gifts. It's designed to complement the digital experience and make learning tangible and hands-on.
