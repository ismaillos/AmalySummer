Our Story

We Build the Next Generation of Changemakers

AMALY Universe is a learning platform designed to help children aged 6–14 discover who they are, how the world works, and how they can make it better.

Our Mission

Education That Transforms, Not Just Informs

We believe every child carries a unique potential that, when nurtured with the right environment, mentorship, and challenges, can transform not just their own life — but the lives of those around them.

AMALY Universe is built on the intersection of character education, cognitive development, creative expression, and global citizenship — five pillars that together create the foundation for a life well-lived.

Our Values

What We Stand For

These values guide everything we create at AMALY — from curriculum design to community culture.

🌱

Growth Mindset

We believe every child can learn and grow. Effort matters more than talent. Failure is a teacher, not a verdict.

🎨

Creative Freedom

Children learn best when they create. We give them space to imagine, experiment, and build things they're proud of.

🌍

Global Citizenship

Our children are citizens of the world. We teach them to see beyond borders and understand their responsibility to others.

🤝

Community First

Learning is social. We build communities where children support each other, celebrate together, and grow together.

🏛️

Rooted in Heritage

We honor where children come from. Cultural identity is a strength, and we celebrate the richness of Moroccan and Arab heritage.

⚡

Excellence in All Things

We don't settle for average. Every workshop, mission, and interaction is designed with care and intention.
