Our Story

We Build the Next Generation of Changemakers

AMALY Universe is a learning platform designed to help children aged 6–14 discover who they are, how the world works, and how they can make it better.

Our Mission

We Shape Who Children Become, Not Just What They Know

AMALY Universe is a hybrid transmedia learning ecosystem — integrating live sessions, digital missions, physical kits, creative projects, and community to cultivate children's identity, values, thinking, creativity, and civic engagement.

Our approach is grounded in evidence-based pedagogies: constructivism, project-based learning, socio-emotional learning (SEL), and identity-based learning — frameworks endorsed by UNESCO Futures of Education and OECD Education 2030.

The curriculum starts with the self — identity, family, values — then expands outward to the world. Because children need self-understanding to ground their learning, and character to make their impact meaningful.

Our Pedagogical Principles

What We Believe About Learning

These principles shape every mission, workshop, and project at AMALY — drawn from decades of research in child development and the science of learning.

🌱

Identity Comes First

Learning is most powerful when children know who they are. We start with the self — identity, values, heritage — then expand outward to the world. (Reggio Emilia, culturally responsive pedagogy)

🎨

Children Learn by Doing

Constructivism and project-based learning show that children build real understanding through making, creating, and solving — not listening. We give them projects they're proud of.

🌍

Morocco & the World

We are rooted in Moroccan and Arab heritage, open to every culture. Global citizenship is not about losing who you are — it's about knowing yourself well enough to connect with everyone.

🤝

Learning Is Social

SEL meta-analyses show sustained improvements in skills, attitudes, and academic outcomes when children learn in connected communities. We build those communities intentionally.

🏛️

Mastery Over Speed

Bloom's mastery learning model shows that when children receive formative feedback and progress at their pace, outcomes improve by up to 2 standard deviations. We believe in depth over coverage.

⚡

Growth Mindset & Resilience

We praise effort and strategy, not talent. Reflection prompts ("What did you try differently?") and safe failure build the resilience that carries children far beyond the summer.
